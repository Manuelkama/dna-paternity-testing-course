# Simulated data

All data in this directory must be fictional and clearly labeled as simulated.

## Data policy

- Never commit identifiable genetic data, real case files, names, dates of birth, contact details, or chain-of-custody records from real cases.
- Do not upload human genetic data unless its public release, consent, licensing, and privacy status have been explicitly reviewed and documented.
- Every generated dataset must include its assumptions, schema, and intended educational use.
- Simulated data must never be represented as evidence from an actual laboratory.

## First fictional example: one STR-like locus

| Person | Allele 1 | Allele 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mother | 8 | 11 |
| Alleged father | 12 | 14 |
| Child | 11 | 12 |

This is a deliberately simple inheritance illustration. It is not a real STR dataset, does not use real population frequencies, and cannot be used to calculate a valid paternity index.

The child's allele 11 is compatible with maternal transmission, and allele 12 is compatible with transmission from the alleged father. Compatibility at one locus is not proof of paternity.
