# References and external learning resources

This page links to original providers. Always check the current version, license, intended use, and applicability before relying on a resource.

## Genetics and molecular biology

- [OpenStax Biology 2e](https://openstax.org/details/books/biology-2e) — free introductory biology textbook.
- [OpenLearn: Inheritance of characters](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/science-maths-technology/biology/inheritance-characters/content-section-0) — free introductory learning material.
- [NCBI Bookshelf](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/) — scientific books and reviews.

## STR and forensic genetics

- [NIST STRBase](https://strbase.nist.gov/) — STR marker reference information and forensic genetics resources.
- [NIST Forensic Genetics](https://www.nist.gov/spo/forensic-science-program/forensic-genetics) — forensic genetics research and resources.
- [OSIRIS source repository](https://github.com/iris-codes/OSIRIS) — software for review and analysis of forensic DNA electrophoresis data. Follow the repository's official documentation and license.
- [Biopython](https://github.com/biopython/biopython) — general biological computation in Python; not a paternity interpretation package.

## Relationship testing and quality

- [AABB Relationship Testing Laboratories](https://www.aabb.org/standards-accreditation/standards/relationship-testing-laboratories) — standards and accreditation information. Full standards may require purchase or member access.
- [AABB Relationship Testing Collection Training](https://www.aabb.org/education-career/education/certificate-programs/relationship-testing-collection-training-module) — collection training information.

## Laboratory manufacturer documentation

For extraction kits, quantification assays, multiplex STR kits, and capillary electrophoresis instruments, use the current official instructions for the exact product and instrument used by the supervised laboratory. Do not substitute protocols from another kit or instrument.

## Citation and version discipline

For every resource used in a lesson, record:
- Organization or author.
- Exact title.
- Edition or version, if available.
- URL and access date.
- License or access conditions, if applicable.
- The specific lesson objective it supports.

This repository does not reproduce paid standards, proprietary manuals, or third-party course content.
